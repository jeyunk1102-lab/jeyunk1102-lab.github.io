High-Protein

Tuna & Oyster Sauce Oil Pasta

"Rich umami from oyster sauce meets savory tuna for a fail-proof, high-protein pasta."
Prep: 10 mins
Cook: 10 mins
Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Main: Pasta noodles, 1 small can of tuna
  • Topping: Broccoli, 2 eggs
  • Sauce: Olive oil (generous amount), oyster sauce, lemon juice, salt, black pepper

Instructions

  1. Step 0 (Prep): Boil pasta noodles until al dente. Lightly blanch broccoli pieces in boiling water.
  2. Step 1 (Toss): Transfer cooked pasta to a pan, drizzle generously with olive oil, and toss over medium heat.
  3. Step 2 (Saute): Add blanched broccoli and oyster sauce (about two coin-sized squirts). Toss drained tuna with lemon juice and black pepper, then saute together briefly.
  4. Step 3 (Finish): Slice soft-boiled eggs in half and set them on top so the golden yolk flows over the pasta. Season with salt if needed.
Pro Tip: A splash of fresh lemon juice removes any fishiness from the canned tuna and brightens the whole dish.
