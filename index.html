High-Protein / Flour-Free

1. Real Oat Protein Pancakes

"No flour needed! Naturally sweet, nutty, and flour-free healthy pancakes."

Ingredients

  • Base: Whole rolled oats, 1 ripe banana
  • Protein: 1–2 eggs, 1–2 tbsp chocolate protein powder
  • Mix: Water (for adjusting consistency)

Instructions

  • Step 0 (Prep): Add ~1/2 cup of rolled oats to a blender. Blend for 15–20 seconds until finely ground into oat flour. Measure out 4 tbsp for use.
  • Step 1 (Batter): Mash the banana in a bowl. Add 4 tbsp oat flour, 1–2 tbsp protein powder, and eggs. Mix well, adding water a spoon at a time until thick and scooping.
  • Step 2 (Cook): Lightly coat a pan with oil and wipe excess with a paper towel. Cook over low heat and flip when bubbles pop on the surface.
Pro Tip (Oat Flour): Shake the blender occasionally while grinding to prevent machine heat from clumping the oats.
High-Protein / Pasta

2. Tuna & Oyster Sauce Oil Pasta

"Rich umami from oyster sauce meets savory tuna for a fail-proof, high-protein pasta." (Serves 2)

Ingredients

  • Main: Pasta noodles, 1 small can of tuna, broccoli, 2 eggs
  • Sauce: Olive oil (generous amount), oyster sauce, lemon juice, salt, black pepper

Instructions

  • Step 0 (Prep): Boil pasta noodles until al dente. Lightly blanch broccoli pieces in boiling water.
  • Step 1 (Toss): Transfer cooked pasta to a pan, drizzle generously with olive oil, and toss over medium heat.
  • Step 2 (Saute): Add blanched broccoli and oyster sauce (about two coin-sized squirts). Toss drained tuna with lemon juice and black pepper, then saute together briefly.
  • Step 3 (Finish): Slice soft-boiled eggs in half and set them on top so the yolk flows over the pasta. Season with salt if needed.
Pro Tip (Flavor Secret): A splash of fresh lemon juice removes any fishiness from the canned tuna and brightens the whole dish.
Light Meal / Bruschetta

3. Garlic Tomato Bruschetta

"Bursting with fresh garlic aroma and bright tomato flavor for a light, healthy meal." (Serves 4)

Ingredients

  • Base: 4 baguette slices, 1–2 fresh garlic cloves
  • Topping: 1 fresh tomato, olive oil, salt
  • Options: Avocado or smoked salmon, red wine vinegar, black pepper, dried oregano

Instructions

  • Step 0 (Prep): Grate the tomato to extract its fresh juice, then whisk with olive oil, salt, and a splash of red wine vinegar.
  • Step 1 (Toast): Drizzle baguette slices with olive oil and toast on a pan until golden and crispy.
  • Step 2 (Infuse): Gently rub a raw garlic clove directly over the warm, toasted bread surface to infuse the garlic aroma.
  • Step 3 (Assemble): Spoon the tomato mixture over the bread, top with avocado or smoked salmon, and sprinkle with pepper and oregano.
Pro Tip (Garlic Control): The raw garlic rubs off very strongly on the first slice, so use gentle pressure when coating.
Gratin / Comfort Food

4. Tomato Basil Dumpling Gratin

"A fancy upgrade for frozen dumplings! Super easy gratin rivaling classic lasagna."

Ingredients

  • Main: Frozen dumplings (6–8 pcs), shrimp (5–6 pcs), tomato pasta sauce
  • Flavor: Basil pesto, shredded cheese (mozzarella/cheddar), minced garlic

Instructions

  • Step 0 (Prep): Pan-fry dumplings until golden. In the same pan, saute minced garlic and shrimp until fragrant.
  • Step 1 (Sauce): Pour tomato sauce into the pan and let it simmer. Toss in the dumplings to coat them evenly.
  • Step 2 (Build): Layer dumplings and sauce in a baking dish, tucking cheese into all the gaps. Place shrimp on the very top.
  • Step 3 (Bake): Air-fry or bake at 180–200°C (350–390°F) for 5–10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
Pro Tip (Meat Sauce Hack): Mash 1–2 dumplings into the tomato sauce to create a rich, bolognese-like texture! Always dot basil pesto on after baking to preserve its vibrant green color.
Sandwich / High-Protein

5. Carrot Râpées & Egg Sandwich

"Crispy, creamy, and colorful—the ultimate aesthetic high-protein sandwich."

Ingredients

  • Main: 2 carrots, bread slices, 2 eggs, perilla leaves (or basil), cream cheese
  • Râpées Dressing: 2 tbsp olive oil, 1.5 tbsp vinegar, 1 tbsp mustard, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 pinch salt, milk (optional)

Instructions

  • Step 0 (Prep): Finely shred carrots and toss with a pinch of salt. Whisk dressing ingredients, toss with carrots, and chill in the fridge.
  • Step 1 (Eggs): Whisk eggs with a splash of milk and salt. Softly scramble over low heat and shape into the size of your bread slice.
  • Step 2 (Spread): Toast bread slices and spread a generous layer of cream cheese. Place aromatic perilla leaves on top.
  • Step 3 (Layer): Heap the chilled carrot râpées and warm egg patty onto the bread, close the sandwich, and slice in half.
Pro Tip (Egg Alternative): Mashed soft-boiled eggs mixed with a touch of mayo also work wonderfully as a creamy filler instead of scrambled eggs.
Gourmet / Low-Carb

6. Abalone & Perilla Oil Soy Noodle Soba

"Light, refreshing, and high in protein—a gourmet meal with abalone and rich perilla oil."

Ingredients

  • Main: 1 pack soy protein noodles, fresh abalone
  • Veggies: Zucchini, green onions
  • Sauce: 2 tbsp Japanese Tsuyu, 1 tbsp toasted perilla oil

Instructions

  • Step 0 (Prep): Rinse soy protein noodles thoroughly in cold water and drain well in a colander. Julienne zucchini and chop green onions.
  • Step 1 (Saute): Lightly saute zucchini to bring out its natural sweetness. Briefly stir-fry green onions. Pan-sear abalone until golden and slice.
  • Step 2 (Mix): Place noodles in a bowl, drizzle with 2 tbsp Tsuyu and 1 tbsp perilla oil, and toss with sauteed veggies.
  • Step 3 (Finish): Top with grilled abalone slices and serve immediately.
Pro Tip (Noodle Prep): Ensure the soy protein noodles are completely drained of excess water so the rich perilla oil and tsuyu flavor won't be diluted.
